UK GDPR & PECR Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who We Are

MultiPro Digital (multiprodigital.co.uk) provides web design, interactive pricing estimator development, and local Google Business Profile optimisation exclusively for UK-registered corporate surface contractors (Limited companies and LLPs). Contact: mahabub@multiprodigital.co.uk.

2. B2B Outreach & Lawful Basis (PECR & UK GDPR)

Where we contact UK Limited companies or LLPs (corporate subscribers under Regulation 22 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 — PECR) regarding relevant web and local search services, we rely on Legitimate Interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR.

  • We only source publicly available corporate business details from company websites, Google Business Profiles, and Companies House.
  • We do not intentionally send unsolicited marketing emails to sole traders or unincorporated partnerships.
  • Every B2B message includes clear sender identification and an immediate opt-out instruction (simply replying "NO").

3. Website Audit Forms

When you submit our 60-second video audit form, we collect your name, company name, work email, town/county, trade, and website URL solely to prepare and deliver your requested audit and reply to your enquiry.

4. Your Rights & Instant Opt-Out

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or object to our processing of your business contact details at any time. To be permanently added to our suppression list within 24 hours, simply reply "NO" to any email from us or write to mahabub@multiprodigital.co.uk.
